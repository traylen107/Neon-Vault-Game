# 💳 NEON VAULT: CREDIT QUEST

### Build your balance. Complete missions. Upgrade your digital empire.

Welcome to **NEON VAULT**, a futuristic credit-card simulation game where you earn virtual credits, complete missions, purchase upgrades, unlock achievements, and customize your own digital credit card.

Think you can build the ultimate vault? Let's find out!

---

## 🚀 GETTING STARTED

When you start playing, you receive your own fictional digital credit card and a starting balance of **900 credits**.

Your goal is to earn as many credits as possible, purchase upgrades, unlock rewards, and progress through the game.

Everything uses virtual game currency. No real money is involved.

## 📋 HOW TO COMPLETE TASKS

Tasks are one of the main ways to earn credits.

1. Open the **Earn Credits** section.
2. Choose an available mission.
3. Click **Start Task** to open the associated external website.
4. Explore the website and read the relevant information.
5. Return to NEON VAULT and answer the mission's questions.
6. Answer the questions correctly to advance through the mission.
7. Complete the entire quiz to receive your reward!

**Important:** Starting a task does not automatically award credits. You must complete its in-game knowledge checks to earn the reward.

External websites are independently operated. The game uses its quizzes to check your understanding, but it cannot automatically verify everything you do on those websites.

## 💰 EARNING CREDITS

There are several ways to grow your balance:

- **Missions:** Complete available tasks and earn rewards.
- **Daily Rewards:** Return regularly to claim available daily bonuses.
- **Streaks:** Keep your activity streak going to work toward additional rewards.
- **Achievements:** Reach milestones and unlock accomplishments.
- **Passive Income:** Purchase available upgrades that help generate credits over time.
- **Boosts:** Activate eligible multipliers to increase rewards.

Keep progressing, earn XP, and level up as you build your virtual fortune!

## 🛍️ THE CREDIT STORE

Visit the store to spend the credits you have earned.

Depending on the available items, you can purchase:

- **Card Skins:** Give your digital card a new appearance.
- **Credit Boosts:** Multiply eligible rewards for a limited time.
- **Permanent Upgrades:** Improve your earning potential.
- **Collectibles:** Expand your collection and personalize your experience.

Always check an item's price and effects before purchasing it.

## 💳 YOUR DIGITAL CREDIT CARD

Your fictional card is the centerpiece of your vault.

You can use the available card controls to:

- Reveal or hide your virtual card number.
- Copy the number using the game's copy feature.
- Freeze or unfreeze your card.
- Customize your card's appearance by unlocking skins.

Your digital card is for the game only. It is not a real bank card and cannot be used for real purchases.

**Never enter actual banking information or real payment-card details into the game.**

## 🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & PROGRESSION

As you play, work toward achievements and milestones.

Complete missions, collect rewards, make purchases, earn experience, and improve your balance to progress.

Keep exploring the game to discover what you can unlock!

## 💾 SAVING YOUR PROGRESS

Your game progress is saved locally in your browser.

For the best experience:
- Use the same browser and device whenever possible.
- Avoid clearing the browser's site data if you want to preserve your save.
- Use the game's save-export feature in Settings when available to keep a backup.

**Please note:** Local saves do not automatically synchronize between different browsers or devices. Clearing your browser data may erase your progress.

## ❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

**A task website did not open.**  
Check your browser's pop-up settings, or try starting the task again.

**I answered a question incorrectly.**  
Read the question again and try to identify the correct answer. Incorrect answers should not advance mission progress.

**My credits did not increase.**  
Check whether you have completed every question in the mission and claimed the reward, if required.

**My progress disappeared.**  
Check whether you are using the same browser and device you used previously. Restoring a lost local save may not be possible without a backup.
